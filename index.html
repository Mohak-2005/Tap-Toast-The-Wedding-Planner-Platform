Client Evaluation Guide & Key Test Scenarios

Quick guide to test the standout interactive features built into this demo:

🤖 1. Joey AI Wedding Assistant
Open the chatbot in Guest Mobile or Couple Dashboard. Joey greets you warmly. Type "change me diet to vegetarion" to see the interactive Accept / Reject / Correct confirmation card! Ask "where do I park" or "what table am I seated at".
🪑 2. Drag & Drop Circular Seating
In Couple Dashboard or MC Pro, navigate to the Seating tab. Drag unassigned guests into tables, seat/unseat guests, create new tables, or click Suggest Seating to auto-fill tables.
💌 3. Digital Thank-You Loving Templates
In Couple Dashboard > Gift & Go tab, click the pencil icon next to any gift with "Due" status. A popup opens with pre-written loving message templates and a custom text editor.
📍 4. Venue & Travel Logistics
In Couple Dashboard or MC Pro, view the dedicated Venue & Travel tab with full address, parking details, transport options, and one-click Google Maps redirection to Fernwood Estate.
