Client Evaluation Guide & Key Test Scenarios

Quick guide to test the standout interactive features built into this demo:

🤖 1. Joey AI Wedding Assistant
Open the chatbot in Guest Mobile or Couple Dashboard. Joey greets you warmly. Type "change me diet to vegetarion" to see the interactive Accept / Reject / Correct confirmation card! Ask "where do I park" or "what table am I seated at".
🪑 2. Drag & Drop Circular Seating
In Couple Dashboard or MC Pro, navigate to the Seating tab. Drag unassigned guests into tables, seat/unseat guests, create new tables, or click Suggest Seating to auto-fill tables.
💌 3. Digital Thank-You Loving Templates
In Couple Dashboard > Gift & Go tab, click the pencil icon next to any gift with "Due" status. A popup opens with pre-written loving message templates and a custom text editor.
📍 4. Venue & Travel Logistics
In Couple Dashboard or MC Pro, view the dedicated Venue & Travel tab with full address, parking details, transport options, and one-click Google Maps redirection to Fernwood Estate.
📖 5. Reusable Content Pages (Shared Editor)
In Couple Dashboard > Content Pages, edit Our Story, In Memoriam, Dining & Menu, Meet the Team, and Honeymoon Updates. Switch live previews and toggle publish status to appear live in Guest Web & Mobile!
🎙️ 6. Private MC Run Sheet (High Priority)
In MC Pro, click on any wedding (e.g. Sophie & James) or the 📋 Run Sheet action to open their dedicated Private MC Run Sheet. View minute cues with editable time, activity, public notes and confidential MC cues. Click ▲/▼ to reorder items, delete or add custom cues with strict authorization restrictions (no AI scheduling).
💌 7. Thank-You Manager (Manual Tracking)
In Couple Dashboard > Gift & Thank-You > Thank-You Manager, track gift and Love Note thank-you statuses: Not Started, Drafted, Sent, and Completed with progress bars and status filters.
🔒 8. Wedding Vault & 1st Anniversary
In Couple Dashboard, open Wedding Vault to inspect the read-only storage allocation, 1-year retention expiry, access controls & safe deletion. Then open 1st Anniversary to view milestone greetings, traditional/modern themes, and 6 memory links!
